# Making a study plan

## Choose one goal

Pick one topic to learn this week. Write the goal in a short sentence. A clear
goal helps you decide what to study next.

## Make a small schedule

Choose a few short study times that fit your week. Put each time on a calendar.
Leave space between study sessions so you can rest and review.

## Review your progress

At the end of the week, check what you learned. Keep what worked and change
one thing that made studying difficult. Then choose the next small goal.
