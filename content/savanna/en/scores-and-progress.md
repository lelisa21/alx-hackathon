# Scores, progress and graduation

## What score do I need to pass a course?

You need a score of 70% or higher. This score earns your course badge. It also unlocks the next course in your programme.

## How is my overall score calculated?

Your overall score is a weighted average of graded tasks. Each graded task has a weight. A weekly task has a weight of 1%. A graded assignment has a weight of five and a half percent. A test has a weight of seven and a half percent.

## Where do I see my score details?

Select See score details under Your overall progress. A window lists every graded task. It shows the week, the weight and your score for each task.

## What does slightly behind mean?

Slightly behind means your progress is below the expected pace. Your overall progress is compared with the course calendar. Finish your current deliverables first to catch up.

## What is the course progression gauge?

A self-paced course shows a course progression gauge. It tracks your journey from enrollment to certification. The gauge marks the 70% score you need for your badge.

## What are legacy points?

Legacy points are rewards for good learning habits. Your dashboard shows the legacy points you have earned. The pacing boost can give you extra legacy points.

## What is the pacing boost?

The pacing boost is a time challenge in self-paced courses. Complete your course before the timer ends. You then earn 25 legacy points. The card shows how many days are left.

## How do I mark a concept as complete?

Read the concept page to the end. Select the Mark as complete button at the bottom. The navigation panel then shows the concept as read.
