# Making a study plan

## How do I make a study plan?

Pick one topic to learn this week. Write the goal in one short sentence. A clear goal helps you decide what to study next.

## How do I make a small study schedule?

Choose a few short study times that fit your week. Put each time on your calendar. Leave space between sessions to rest and review.

## How do I review my progress?

At the end of the week, check what you learned. Keep what worked. Change one thing that made studying hard. Then choose your next small goal.

## What should I do if I fall behind?

Do not try to finish everything in one day. Start with your immediate deliverables. Ask for help early if you feel stuck.
