# WR 100: Fundamentals of Effective Communication

## What is WR 100 about?

WR 100 teaches clear and effective professional communication. You learn to write, edit and present ideas with clarity. You also learn to use AI tools responsibly in communication.

## How long does WR 100 last?

WR 100 is a paced course of 15 weeks. The cohort runs from 31 August to 20 December 2026. Each week has its own tasks and deadlines.

## What happens in week 1 of WR 100?

Week 1 is called Fixing the Message: A Communication Makeover. You improve an unclear message step by step. You learn why clear messages save time.

## What is the feedback loop in week 2?

Week 2 is called The Feedback Loop. You learn to give and receive useful feedback. You also use AI to improve your drafts.

## What do I learn about meetings in week 3?

Week 3 is called Breaking the Barriers. It moves from messy meetings to executive clarity. You learn to summarise meetings into clear actions.

## How do I use AI as a sounding board?

Week 4 is called Stress-Testing Logic. You use AI as a strategic sounding board. Ask AI to question your argument and find weak points. You still decide what is true and useful.

## How do I build a logical argument?

Week 5 is called Thesis to Architecture. Start with one clear thesis sentence. Then add points that support it in a logical order.

## How do I edit my writing professionally?

Week 6 is called Precision and Flow: The Professional Edit. Remove words that add no meaning. Check that each sentence leads to the next one.

## How do I write shorter messages?

Week 7 is called The Brevity Benchmark. You cut corporate noise from your writing. Use short sentences and common words. Put the main point first.

## How do I verify content generated by AI?

Week 10 is about verifying AI-generated content. AI can produce errors that look correct. Check facts, numbers and names against trusted sources. Never submit AI content you have not checked.
