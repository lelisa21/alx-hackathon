# Getting help with Dimts

## What is Dimts?

Dimts is the learning assistant inside Savanna. It answers questions from your course material. It speaks English, Amharic and Afaan Oromoo.

## How do I ask Dimts a question?

Select the Open Dimts button at the bottom of the page. Type your question and press Send. Dimts answers and shows the lesson it used.

## Can I ask Dimts a question by voice?

Yes, if your browser supports voice for your language. Select Start talking and speak your question. Check the text, then select Send.

## How do I talk to a real person?

Select the Help me now button. Dimts sends your request to a helper. You can keep studying while you wait.

## Can I use Dimts on Telegram?

Yes. Open the Dimts bot on Telegram. Send start, then choose your language. Ask your question in a normal message.

## Can I use Dimts by SMS?

Yes. Send your question by text message to the Dimts number. Each reply is short to fit one message. Send STOP to end messages.

## Why does Dimts say it did not find an answer?

Dimts answers only from your course material. It does not guess. Try other words, or ask a person for help.

## Is my conversation private?

Dimts does not show your name to helpers. Remote AI is used only if you allow it. Select Delete my data in Settings to remove your chat.
