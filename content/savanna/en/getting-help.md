# Getting help with Bridgely

## What is Bridgely?

Bridgely is the learning assistant inside Savanna. It answers questions from your course material. It speaks English, Amharic, Afaan Oromoo and Tigrinya.

## How do I ask Bridgely a question?

Select the Open Bridgely button at the bottom of the page. Type your question and press Send. Bridgely answers and shows the lesson it used.

## Can I ask Bridgely a question by voice?

Yes, if your browser supports voice for your language. Select Start talking and speak your question. Check the text, then select Send.

## How do I talk to a real person?

Select the Help me now button. Bridgely sends your request to a helper. You can keep studying while you wait.

## Can I use Bridgely on Telegram?

Yes. Open the Bridgely bot on Telegram. Send start, then choose your language. Ask your question in a normal message.

## Can I use Bridgely by SMS?

Yes. Send your question by text message to the Bridgely number. Each reply is short to fit one message. Send STOP to end messages.

## Why does Bridgely say it did not find an answer?

Bridgely answers only from your course material. It does not guess. Try other words, or ask a person for help.

## Is my conversation private?

Bridgely does not show your name to helpers. Remote AI is used only if you allow it. Select Delete my data in Settings to remove your chat.
