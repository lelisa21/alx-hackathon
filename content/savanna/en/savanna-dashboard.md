# Using your Savanna dashboard

## What is the Savanna dashboard?

Savanna is the ALX learning platform. Your dashboard is the Home page. It shows your course, your tasks and your events. Open it first each time you study.

## How do I continue where I left off?

Find the card called Continue where you left off. Select the Resume button on that card. Savanna opens the lesson you used last.

## Where do I see my immediate deliverables?

Look for the section called Your immediate deliverables. It lists the tasks due in the current week. Each task shows its week, its weight and its progress. Select Resume to open a task.

## How do I switch to another course?

Open the My courses dropdown at the top of the page. It lists every course in your programme. Select a course to open its dashboard.

## How do I use the week strip?

A paced course shows a strip of weeks near the top. Select a week to see its tasks and lessons. Use the arrows to scroll to earlier or later weeks. The current week is highlighted.

## Where are my upcoming events?

The section Your upcoming events lists live sessions and workshops. Each event shows its date and its time. Select See all my events for the full calendar.

## How do I collapse the sidebar menu?

Select the menu button with three lines at the top. The sidebar shrinks to a narrow row of icons. Select it again to show the full menu. Savanna remembers your choice.

## What is in the sidebar menu?

The sidebar links to Home, My courses and Concepts. It also links to Projects, Peers and My Profile. Use Video on demand to watch recorded sessions.
